This weekend, I built 'Kirby, a custom AI assistant powered by Hermes via OpenRouter. The system integrates top-tier LLMs—including DeepSeek V4, Grok 4.7, GLM 5.2, and Gemini 3.7 Flash and features a custom Discord bot interface for seamless daily interaction. I look forward to testing its performance and reliability in the coming weeks.
